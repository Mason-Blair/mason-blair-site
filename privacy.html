Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026

Mason Blair Accountancy Services ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Who we are

Mason Blair Accountancy Services. Contact: info@mason-blair.com.

What we collect

When you use our contact form or email us, we collect your name, email address, the service you are interested in, and the content of your message.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to provide our services. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Legal basis

We process your information because it is necessary to respond to your request and take steps before entering into a contract, and for our legitimate interests in running our business.

Who we share it with

Form submissions are processed by our form provider (Formspree) and delivered to our email. We may also share information with professional advisers or authorities where legally required.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are kept for up to 12 months. Client records are kept for the periods required by law and professional regulations.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email us to make a request. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.
