Common questions

Do I need an accountant as a sole trader?

You don't have to have one, but many sole traders find it saves time, reduces mistakes and helps them keep more of what they earn. I can help with as much or as little as you need.

When is my Self Assessment tax return due?

For most people the online filing deadline is 31 January following the end of the tax year (which ends 5 April), and any tax owed is also due by then. Check GOV.UK for the current year's exact dates.

What is Making Tax Digital for Income Tax?

It's a change to how some sole traders and landlords keep records and report to HMRC, being phased in by income level. If it applies to you, you'll need to keep digital records and send regular updates using compatible software. Check GOV.UK to see whether and when it applies to you, and get in touch if you'd like help getting ready.

What do I need to send you?

Typically your income and expense records (or bank statements), plus any relevant invoices and receipts. I'll give you a simple checklist once we've spoken.

Can you take over from my current accountant?

Yes. Get in touch and I'll explain what's needed to make the switch smooth.
