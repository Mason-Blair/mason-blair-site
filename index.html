Common questions

Do I need an accountant as a sole trader?

You don't have to have one, but many sole traders find it saves time, reduces mistakes and helps them keep more of what they earn. We can help with as much or as little as you need.

When is my Self Assessment tax return due?

For most people the online filing deadline is 31 January following the end of the tax year (which ends 5 April), and any tax owed is also due by then. Check GOV.UK for the current year's exact dates.

What do I need to send you?

Typically your income and expense records (or bank statements), plus any relevant invoices and receipts. We'll give you a simple checklist once we've spoken.
